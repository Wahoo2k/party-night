Questions

Good to know

What do I need to play?

A TV or computer screen that can open a web page, and a phone for each player. The easiest setup is a laptop plugged into the TV. TV apps for Google TV, Fire TV and Roku are planned.

Do players need to install an app or make an account?

No. Players scan the code on the TV, or type the four-letter room code, and enter a name. That is all.

How many people can play?

It depends on the game. Most play with 3 to 12, some with as few as 2 and some up to 20. Each game card shows its range.

Is it okay for kids?

Yes. Kids mode keeps prompts family-safe, and many games need no reading at all. The Family Pack is built for mixed ages.

Can someone join from another house?

Yes. A far-away player opens the remote screen link to watch the TV picture on their own device and plays from their phone. Use a video call for voices. A few games that need everyone in one room are labelled.

Who has to pay?

Only the person hosting. Players never pay. Four games are free forever, and every other game can be played once before you decide.

Does one purchase work on all my screens?

Yes. What you buy belongs to your account, not to one device. Link the TV, tablet, laptop or phone you host from (up to 10) and they all play what you own.

I was given a gift code. How do I use it?

Open the account page, sign in with your email, and type the code under "Have a gift code?". No card is needed.

What is the difference between Everything and the Party Pass?

Everything is a one-time purchase of all the games available today, yours to keep. The Party Pass is a subscription that includes every game, plus new games and packs that arrive while you subscribe.

Can I buy it yet?

Not yet. Buying opens soon, and every game is open to try in the meantime. Email support@toolshedapps.com for a note when it launches.

Does it need the internet?

Yes. The TV and the phones talk to each other over the internet, so everyone needs a connection.

Something went wrong. Who do I contact?

Email support@toolshedapps.com and say which game you were playing and what happened.
